# Limitations log

| ID | Limitation | Consequence | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | 2011 has only 15 cities | Low power and unstable asymptotics | Effect sizes, permutation p, bootstrap CI, leave-one-out |
| L2 | Mapping estimates are not census counts | Outcome error may differ by city | Avoid prevalence language; interpret as mapped density |
| L3 | No published city density in acquired 2006–07, 2014, or Round 5 sources | Density cannot be replicated honestly | Primary density analysis only in 2011 |
| L4 | Round 5 report lacks city typology composition | Structural replication unavailable | Context-only Round 5 counts |
| L5 | City outcome versus district urban exposure | Geographic misclassification | Explicit grades; all-area and close-match sensitivities |
| L6 | PSLM economic-situation item is subjective | May capture expectations as well as material loss | Triangulate with poverty, illiteracy, sanitation |
| L7 | SPDC poverty estimates are model-based | Additional estimation error is not propagated | Secondary only; describe method |
| L8 | LFS annual reports yield national/provincial estimates | Cannot support city/district predictor | Period context only |
| L9 | Typology definitions and mapping intensity may vary by round | Raw pooled comparison may be misleading | Round-specific analyses; cautious rank-change analysis |
| L10 | Administrative names and boundaries change | Potential join error | Explicit versioned crosswalk and rename notes |
| L11 | Urban distress response distributions shifted across rounds | Raw change scores mix scale shift with local change | Within-round percentile ranks for exploratory change |
| L12 | Count-based scale adjustment is endogenous to density | Possible overadjustment/mathematical coupling | Sensitivity only, clearly caveated |
| L13 | Round 5's 18 printed city estimates sum to 71,315, not its printed Grand Total of 64,829 | National Round 5 total is internally inconsistent | Preserve city rows; flag discrepancy; context only |
