# Source notes

## Surveillance and mapping

- **2006–07 Round II mapping report:** city typology counts for 12 cities; no city density per 1,000 adult men in the acquired report.
- **2011 Round IV mapping report and Emmanuel et al. article/XML:** 15-city totals, typology counts/shares, and published density per 1,000 adult men. This is the primary outcome source.
- **2014 Punjab IBBS report:** typology counts for Lahore, Faisalabad, Multan, and Sargodha; no adult-male density.
- **2015–16 mapping excerpt and 2016–17 Round V IBBS report:** Round V city totals are published, but acquired materials do not provide city density or city typology composition. The 18 printed city averages sum to 71,315, whereas the table's printed Grand Total is 64,829 (difference 6,486). The repository preserves the city rows, flags the internal inconsistency, and uses the round as context only.

## Economic and social indicators

- **PSLM district reports (2006–07, 2010–11, 2014–15):** primary economic-situation exposure, literacy, and household toilet facilities. Urban-stratum estimates are used for city alignment; all-area estimates are sensitivity measures.
- **SPDC Research Report 85 (2013):** model-based 2010–11 district poverty estimates, including urban and rural components. Secondary 2011 exposure only.
- **LFS annual reports (2010–11, 2014–15, 2017–18):** national/provincial labor-market context. Published designs support national/provincial estimates with urban/rural breakdown, not the city-level predictor required here.
- **LFS 2006–07:** the current PBS publications-page link labeled 2006–07 returned a 2008–09 report during acquisition and was rejected. The old split-results URL was not retrievable from the live site. No 2006–07 LFS statistic enters the dataset.
- **Pakistan Economic Survey overviews:** macroeconomic context only; no city-level variable is merged into the analytical data.

## Provenance

`data/source_manifest.csv` records source URL, role, access status, byte size, and SHA-256 checksum. Table/page locators are carried in processed rows and the data dictionary.
