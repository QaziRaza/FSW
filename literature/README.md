# Paper 1 literature repository

This directory is the reusable literature base for interpreting the Pakistan analysis. It is a **targeted comprehensive review**, not a registered systematic review and not a claim that every paper ever published on sex work and economic conditions was captured.

## What is here

- `review_synthesis.md` — the plain-language answer from the literature and how it changes the interpretation of Paper 1.
- `discussion_comparison_map.md` — finding-by-finding links between our results and sources to cite in a future Discussion.
- `source_summaries.md` — original, abstract-style summaries of every included source, with methods, findings, relevance, limitations, and links.
- `evidence_matrix.csv` — machine-readable study and report ledger.
- `references.bib` — citation-manager-ready BibTeX records.
- `search_protocol.md` — scope, searches, inclusion rules, and the limits of this review.
- `search_log.csv` — reproducible search themes and the reason each was searched.

## How to use it later

1. Start with `discussion_comparison_map.md` and select the row matching the Paper 1 result being discussed.
2. Read the linked entry in `source_summaries.md`; do not cite from the one-line matrix alone.
3. Open the DOI, PubMed, PMC, publisher, or official-report link and verify the exact claim in the source before final manuscript submission.
4. Cite using the `bibkey` in `evidence_matrix.csv` and `references.bib`.

## Important abstract note

Publisher abstracts are linked, not copied wholesale. `source_summaries.md` contains our own concise summaries so the repository remains useful without reproducing copyrighted abstracts. These summaries are evidence notes, not quotations.

## Reading the evidence grades

- **A** — strongest for the specific comparison: official primary surveillance, a strong longitudinal/quasi-experimental design, or a systematic review directly answering the mechanism.
- **B** — useful direct evidence with meaningful design limits, such as qualitative longitudinal work, cross-sectional surveys, or well-constructed narrative synthesis.
- **C** — contextual or hypothesis-generating evidence, including very small qualitative studies and papers with weaker sampling or limited methodological reporting.

The grade is about usefulness for this paper's claim, not an overall judgment on the value of a study.

## Central conclusion

The literature supports a two-sided economic mechanism. Economic deterioration can increase the **supply** of women needing income from sex work, while simultaneously reducing **demand** from clients and pushing activity into less visible, more informal, home-based, phone-mediated, or transactional forms. Recovery can restore demand but also reduce new economically driven entry. Because leaving sex work is often difficult and exit/re-entry cycles are common, improved conditions need not make the existing mapped population fall; a slowdown or plateau is a plausible population-stock response.
