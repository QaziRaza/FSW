# Journal target recommendation for Paper 1

Reviewed 3 October 2026.

## Recommendation

Submit first to **Sexually Transmitted Infections** as an Original Research article using the journal's subscription route.

This is the strongest balance of subject fit, measurable journal standing, plausible editorial interest, and zero mandatory author charge. The journal explicitly covers clinical, epidemiological, and sociological aspects of sexually transmitted infections and HIV. It has already published several of the Pakistani surveillance papers on which this manuscript builds. Its current publisher-reported metrics are an impact factor of 3.0, CiteScore of 4.6, and overall acceptance rate of 37%. The publisher states that authors may publish at no cost under the subscriber-access route and that there are no submission, page, or online-only colour charges.

Official evidence:

- [STI author instructions, scope, limits, data policy and charges](https://sti.bmj.com/pages/authors)
- [STI publisher-reported journal metrics](https://sti.bmj.com/pages/top-cited-articles/)

## Why this is the best first target

1. **Direct subject fit.** The paper concerns female sex work, HIV surveillance, population mapping, structural vulnerability, and policy implications.
2. **Documented Pakistan precedent.** The journal published the 2013 Pakistan surveillance supplement articles cited in the manuscript, including work on organisation, population size, structural vulnerability, surveillance, and heterogeneous HIV trends.
3. **No mandatory APC.** Open access is optional. Selecting the standard subscription route avoids the GBP 3,760 open-access charge.
4. **Better probability signal than the broader alternative.** STI reports a 37% overall acceptance rate. Journal of Epidemiology and Community Health reports 7%.
5. **The required reporting framework is already prepared.** STI requires STROBE for observational studies, and a completed cross-sectional checklist is included in the submission package.

No journal can guarantee acceptance, and the reported acceptance rate is journal-wide rather than specific to ecological studies. The recommendation is therefore the best evidence-based target, not a promise.

## STI formatting profile

The complete manuscript is the authoritative scientific version. STI's Original Research format permits 3,000 main-text words, a 300-word structured abstract, four combined tables or figures, and 30 references, and it requires a three-part key-messages box. The complete master contains approximately 7,582 main-text words, five tables, three figures, and 45 references.

An STI-formatted derivative uses the following editorial priorities without changing the analyses or conclusions:

- retain the primary density result, the acute-versus-chronic scale contrast, and the deterioration-to-recovery panel as the central story;
- keep four display items in the main manuscript and move full regression, sensitivity, network, transition, and macro-marker tables into the supplementary appendix;
- reduce the main reference list to the 30 sources needed for the paper's argument while leaving the complete BibTeX library in the repository;
- add the required key-messages box.

## Recommended submission sequence

1. Sexually Transmitted Infections, using its journal-specific format.
2. AIDS and Behavior, using the comprehensive version with lighter reformatting if STI declines.
3. Journal of Epidemiology and Community Health only if the authors prefer a broader population-health audience despite the lower reported acceptance rate.

Do not select paid open access during submission. At STI, choose the standard exclusive-licence or subscription publication route. Decline any optional print-colour charge; online-only colour is free.
